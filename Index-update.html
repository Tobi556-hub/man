Brief History

Egbe-Idimu Local Council Development Area is a grassroots administrative area in Lagos State, serving communities around Egbe, Idimu and Isheri-Olofin.

The council was established as part of Lagos State's effort to bring governance and development closer to the people at the grassroots level.

Today, the council works with residents and relevant government institutions to support local infrastructure, environmental management, social services and community development.
